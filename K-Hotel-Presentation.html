Delivery

A phased roadmap.

Phase 1 · ~4–6 wks

Launch

  • Brand setup & design
  • Rooms, offers & booking + payment
  • My Reservation & notifications
  • Core dashboard (content, offers, prices, users)
Phase 2 · ~6–10 wks

Engage

  • Dining, spa & events booking
  • Concierge chat & K Rewards
  • 3-hour reminder automation
  • Arabic / RTL + analytics
Phase 3 · later

Scale

  • Mobile check-in & digital key
  • In-app ordering & Wallet pass
  • PMS / channel-manager integration
  • Multi-property rollout
